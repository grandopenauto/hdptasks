Data & Documents

PDF to Excel Extraction

Move useful tables and records out of PDFs and into structured spreadsheet data you can work with.

What you receive

  • Structured Excel or CSV output from the requested PDF content.
  • Field and table organization based on the target format.
  • Validation notes for ambiguous, missing or unreadable values.
  • A clear distinction between extracted values and exceptions.

What we need from you

  • The source PDF.
  • The fields, tables or sections you want extracted.
  • Your preferred output structure and file format.

Good fit for

Statements, reports, price lists, tables, records and recurring documents where manual copy-and-paste is wasting time.

FAQ

Does every PDF extract perfectly?

No. Scans, poor source quality and irregular layouts can create exceptions. Those cases should be surfaced rather than silently guessed.

Can the output follow my existing spreadsheet columns?

Yes. Providing the target columns makes the task more deterministic and easier to validate.
