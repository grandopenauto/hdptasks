Data & Documents

Excel & CSV Cleanup

Turn messy spreadsheet data into a cleaner, more consistent file that is easier to review, analyze or import.

What you receive

  • Cleaned and standardized spreadsheet output.
  • Duplicate handling based on agreed rules.
  • Normalized fields, labels and formatting where appropriate.
  • Processing notes identifying exceptions or assumptions.

What we need from you

  • Your source Excel or CSV file.
  • The structure you want preserved or created.
  • Rules for duplicates, blanks and inconsistent values.
  • Your preferred output format.

Good fit for

CRM exports, customer lists, inventory files, research datasets, vendor lists and operational spreadsheets that need to become usable again.

FAQ

Will the original file be preserved?

The workflow is designed around producing a cleaned output rather than destructively editing the only copy of your source data.

Can custom cleanup rules be used?

Yes. The intake can capture field-specific rules so the cleanup matches how you actually use the data.
