Lead & Prospect Research

Build a focused prospect list around the customers, companies or organizations that actually match your target criteria.

What you receive

  • A structured prospect list matched to your stated criteria.
  • Company and market details useful for qualification.
  • Fit notes explaining why each prospect was included.
  • Research sources where available.

What we need from you

  • Your ideal customer profile.
  • Industry, geography and company-size preferences.
  • Required buying or qualification criteria.
  • Any exclusions or companies to avoid.

Good fit for

Outbound preparation, partner discovery, account research, sales territories and early-stage customer acquisition work.

FAQ

Is this the same as sending outreach?

No. This task produces the researched prospect set. Outreach can be a separate task so targeting and messaging remain independently reviewable.

Can the list be tightly filtered?

Yes. Specific criteria are encouraged because they improve the usefulness of the resulting list.
