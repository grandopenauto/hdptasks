FAQ

Does an audit guarantee higher rankings?

No. The task identifies issues and opportunities; rankings depend on implementation, competition, search behavior and other factors outside a single audit.

Can Search Console data be incorporated?

Yes, when appropriate access or exported data is provided. That can make recommendations much more specific.
