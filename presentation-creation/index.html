Content & Business

Business Presentation

Turn source material, research or an idea into a presentation that is structured around the audience and the decision you need to communicate.

What you receive

  • A clear presentation narrative and slide sequence.
  • Slide-ready headlines, supporting points and calls to action.
  • Audience-appropriate framing.
  • Speaker or delivery notes when requested.
  • Source organization when the presentation is research based.

What we need from you

  • The purpose of the presentation.
  • The intended audience.
  • Your source material or core idea.
  • Desired length and any brand guidance.

Good fit for

Sales presentations, internal proposals, project updates, research briefings, partnership conversations and business concepts that need a clearer narrative.

FAQ

Can you work from rough notes?

Yes. The task can begin from notes, source documents or an existing outline as long as the purpose and audience are clear.

Can research be included?

Yes. If additional factual research is required, that can be defined as part of the task scope or paired with a research task.
